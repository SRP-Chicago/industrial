    SWOT

    SWOT Analysis

    Team to Update

    Strengths

    • Infill West Cook location. 1.2 miles to I-290, 3.3 miles to I-294
    • Strong loading and yard for a building of this age.
    • 100+ trailer positions and outside storage
    • Heavy power and parking
    • Truck repair capability.
    • Size flexibility.
    • Institutional, well-capitalized landlord.
    • Built-out office in most units.

    Weakness

    • Low clear height (18’).
    • Irregular, piecemeal demising.
    • High taxes and operating expenses.
    • Inconsistent and shared loading.
    • Mixed-use adjacencies..

    Opportunities

    • Re-demise into standardized bays.
    • New leasing team to drive fresh activity
    • Flexible deal structuring.
    • Sale to data center companies.
    • Small-bay and last-mile demand.

    Threats

    • Competing vacancy next door (2000 S 25th Ave).
    • Newer, taller product elsewhere.
    • Rollover and re-leasing costs.
    • Aging systems and capital needs.
